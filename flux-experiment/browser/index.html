Why preflight first?

FLUX.1-dev is a different architecture from FLUX.2 Klein and NoobAI/SDXL. We'll check WebGPU limits and FP16 support before choosing an ONNX or custom low-bit runtime. The official ONNX exports are gated and require accepting their Hugging Face conditions.
